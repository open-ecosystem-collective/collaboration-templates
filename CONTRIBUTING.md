# Contributing

These templates get better when the ecosystem works on them together. Every contribution is welcome.

## Lawyers welcome

The templates have **not been legally verified yet**. If you are a lawyer and would like to review them, we are happy to hear from you: [open an issue](https://github.com/open-ecosystem-collective/collaboration-templates/issues). Reviews for Swiss law and for other jurisdictions are equally welcome. With your consent, we name reviewers in the README.

## How to suggest changes

1. **Open an issue** describing what you would change and why.
2. **Or open a pull request** with your change. Keep one topic per pull request.
3. Explain in the description which clause or section is affected and what problem the change solves.

## How we comment

- Every critique comes with a suggestion. Instead of «I don't like this», write «I'd put it like this».
- We trust each other's expertise: sharpen your own field and leave the rest to the people who know it best.

## Writing rules

- Plain, clear English; short sentences.
- No em-dashes; use commas, colons or parentheses.
- No gendered language; use neutral role terms such as «Party», «customer», «partner».
- Placeholders always in `[square brackets]`.
- Recommendations always as quote blocks starting with **OEC recommendation:**.
- No company names, people or real deals: the templates stay fully anonymised.
