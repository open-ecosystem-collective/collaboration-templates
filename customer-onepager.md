## What we promise you

1. **One contract, one point of contact.** You never manage several providers.
2. **You have a say in who leads.** The lead is the partner that fits your project best, not the biggest company.
3. **At most three people at your table.** Tech, sales and project management, aligned.
4. **You hear about changes at once.** Scope, timeline, budget: you hear it from us as soon as we know.
5. **No surprises in the hours.** The budget includes an open buffer, and nothing is booked beyond it without alignment.
6. **You stay free.** Every component is documented and can be replaced on its own. No lock-in.

Every part of your project sits with the people who know it best. When your needs change, a new specialist can join without replacing what already works. And you never depend on a single company.
