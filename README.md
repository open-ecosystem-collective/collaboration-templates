# OEC Collaboration Templates

Open templates from the **Open Ecosystem Collective (OEC)** that help companies work together on customer projects instead of competing for them. Together, the specialists beat the generalist.

> **Not legally verified: have it checked by a lawyer.** These are templates, not finished contracts. They have not been reviewed by a lawyer yet and are not legal advice. Everyone who uses them must show them to their own lawyer before signing and make sure they are correct and fit their situation and jurisdiction.
>
> **Lawyers welcome:** if you are a lawyer and would like to review these templates, we are happy to hear from you: [offer a review](https://github.com/open-ecosystem-collective/collaboration-templates/issues/new?template=legal-review-offer.yml). See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## The templates

| Template | Purpose | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Collaboration Framework Agreement](templates/collaboration-framework-agreement.md) | The basis of the collaboration between two companies | Signed once, at onboarding, always between two Parties |
| [Customer Project Addendum](templates/customer-project-addendum.md) | The «mission report» for one joint customer project: customer, roles, hours, commercial basis | Signed per lead or project, with any number of Parties |
| [Project Coordination Meeting Notes](templates/coordination-meeting-notes.md) | Ready-to-use notes for partner kick-off, pitch preparation, pitch, coordination and closing meetings | Used throughout the project |

## Principles

- **The customer is at the centre.** The customer's needs decide who does what.
- **On the same level.** Every Party can take the lead; the customer has a say in who leads.
- **Everyone does what they do best.** Opportunities go to the partner best placed to serve the customer.
- **Mutual.** All fees, obligations and protection clauses apply in both directions.
- **One team towards the customer.** One contract, one commercial and one technical contact per project, and at most three people in customer meetings.
- **Fairness over control.** The agreements cover what is necessary and rely on each Party's self-accountability.

## How to use

1. Copy the templates you need.
2. Replace every placeholder in `[square brackets]`.
3. Follow or adapt the **OEC recommendations** (quote blocks), then delete them.
4. Keep or delete the clauses marked *optional* and check the numbering.
5. Have the result reviewed by a lawyer before signing.

The templates are written for Swiss law (Swiss Code of Obligations, CO). For other jurisdictions, a legal review is essential.
