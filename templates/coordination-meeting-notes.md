Ready-to-use notes for the meetings of a joint customer project: partner kick-off, pitch preparation, pitch at the customer's site, recurring coordination and closing. The first two meetings are between the partners only; the customer meets all Parties for the first time at the pitch at his premises. Copy the section you need for each meeting, fill it in during the meeting and share it with all Parties within [one (1)] working day.

> **How to use this template:** It belongs to the [OEC Template: Collaboration Framework Agreement](collaboration-framework-agreement.md) and the [OEC Template: Customer Project Addendum](customer-project-addendum.md). The Lead Partner chairs the meetings and keeps the notes. Replace all `[placeholders]`. The tables show Parties A to D: add rows or columns for further Parties, or delete those not needed. Parties A and B are mandatory, there is no maximum.

## Ground rules for every meeting

- **Both SPOCs of every Party attend the internal meetings.** Each Party sends its Sales SPOC and its Technical SPOC to every partner meeting, so that all information is available to everyone (Art. 4.4). Not every Party has to attend: a Party that cannot attend sends a briefed deputy or hands its attendance to another partner it fully trusts, who passes on all information.
- **Max. three people towards the customer.** In customer meetings, including the pitch, the Parties are represented by at most three people in total, ideally tech, sales and project or partner management (Art. 4.5). All SPOCs are briefed before and informed after.
- **Not billable.** Meetings and all other partner effort are not invoiced between the Parties and do not count against the project hour budget. Each Party bears its own time and costs (Art. 7.6).
- **Changes are shared immediately.** Changes to the customer agreement, scope, timeline, budget or contacts go to all Parties as soon as they are known, not only at the next meeting (Art. 4.3).
- **No overbooking.** No Party books hours beyond the agreed budget before this has been aligned in a meeting (Art. 7.5).
- **Every critique comes with a suggestion.** We trust each other's expertise.
- **Report at Party level, never per person.**
